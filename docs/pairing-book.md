# The Pairing Book

*Secular songs paired with kirtans/stotras by shared speech act. A running log — the instrument decides what survives.*

**Method (full reasoning: [The Grid and the Voice](../../eno-workshop/catalogue/stream-C/The-Grid-and-the-Voice.html), Stream C):** a pairing works when the secular hook is a **found translation of the mantra's speech act**. Model: Krishna Das, "Sri Argala Stotram / Show Me Love" (*Kirtan Wallah*) — *rupam dehi* ("show me your form") ⟷ "I want you to show me."

**v2 correction (2026-07-07):** KD paired Foreigner because 70s radio was load-bearing in *his* nervous system. So the songs here come from Thomas's own sonic profile (the Gemini sonic-identity analysis + sonic-profile.md) — the songs that are already in the bones. Generic candidates from v1 (Withers, Beatles, Cohen, Simone) removed; superseded, recoverable from git history.

## The five-layer test

0. **Load-bearing** *(prerequisite)* — the secular song must already matter to *you*. Not a good song: *your* song. (The whole method fails on borrowed material.)
1. **Speech act** *(the engine)* — what is the singing *doing*? Petition · praise · surrender · longing · vow · consolation · invocation · refuge. The mantra's act and the hook's act must match.
2. **Register** — still / heavy-grounded / warm-communal / bright / fierce-driving (open registry; a pairing can reveal a new register — that's how Durga entered).
3. **Form mechanic** — can hook and refrain alternate as call-and-response? **The swap test:** could you exchange the two refrains and lose nothing?
4. **Bench** — singable key (transpose freely), a taal the rig has, playable at current skill level. Arrangement must keep the profile's four criteria: tactile grid (Foundation), warm drone (Climate), space (Shelter), close voice (Connection).

## Working a pairing (instrument-track, no AI)

1. Recreate the song's bed in the set: its vamp on harmonium/synth, its feel on the taal clips. Keep all four profile criteria (grid, drone, space, close voice).
2. Chant the mantra over it. Then sing the hook. Then alternate — hook as the response line.
3. Apply the swap test live. If it holds, log it below with what the instrument revealed.
